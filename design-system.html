2 · Iconography

Phosphor, regular weight, drawn white on a category-coloured disc. One glyph per category, used identically on the map, in chips, and in panel badges.

Do: reuse the category glyph everywhere that category appears.
Don't: introduce a second glyph for the same category, or tint a glyph anything but white.

ZoomControl

One card, hairline dividers. Reset-to-overview moves out of the topbar caret and into this stack (map-trifold glyph) — it is a map control, so it belongs with the map controls. No zoom-level readout: real maps don't use one, and the buttons already dim at each end.

Directory row

The docked panel's rows. Full-width button, 44px minimum, badge · name · count · chevron. One pin flies the map to it; a category with several pins filters the map instead, because there is no single point to fly to.

Stepper

12 of 62 · In the Park

Sits above the title: where you are in the row, then what you are looking at. Booth pitch is ~9 map units, so tapping a specific booth is unreliable even zoomed in. This is the primary way through a row. It wraps inside one area — running off the end of the car-path market returns to its start rather than spilling into the food trucks.

4 · What is defined but not shipped

--currentCoral, reserved for "now". Its only uses were you-are-here and locate-on; GPS was cut for time. Kept so "now" has a colour when something needs one — never as decoration.
.ffc-scaleScale bar. Needs one confirmed ground measurement before it can carry a number.
--font-displayBrice, for the brand lockup. Falls back to Manrope until the licensed file lands — see src/assets/fonts/README.md.

Everything else in this page is live in the app. Tokens that no component reads have been removed rather than left as a menu of options — --surface-panel, --pin-inactive, --you-are-here and the unused brand shades went in the cleanup step.

5 · Accessibility floor

Contrast4.5:1 for text. --text-muted is 5.4:1 on white; the greys it replaces (#9aa0ac, #8b90a0, #8a8f7f) all failed.
Tap44px minimum hit area, even where the visual is 28 or 40.
Type12px floor. No 11px.
SelectionNever opacity alone — a navy fill, or a navy ring where the fill carries category.
Focus2px navy ring, 2px offset, on every interactive element.
Closed UIHidden, not just translated off-screen, so it leaves the tab order.
